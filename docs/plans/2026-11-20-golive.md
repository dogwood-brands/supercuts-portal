### AWS cost decision (2026-11-20)

The **Lean setup, $145/month** (Single-AZ RDS, no WAF) is the chosen direction, per
`CutsHub AWS Costs.md` and Chris's "keep costs as low as possible" — and it can meet the security
and privacy minimums: managed encrypted PostgreSQL in private subnets, point-in-time backups,
Secrets Manager, private S3, ACM TLS, staging/production separation all stay.

What Lean gives up, and when to buy it back:
- **WAF (+$11.20/month)** — edge attack filtering moves to the in-app rate limiter (which the app
  already has). Add WAF before field rollout if the independent security review asks for it.
- **Multi-AZ standby (+≈$26/month)** — failover becomes "restore from backup, typically under an
  hour". Acceptable pre-field-rollout; revisit at rollout when the portal is shift-critical for
  51 salons.
